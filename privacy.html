Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hanzala Auto Poster ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the App. By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account through TikTok's official Login Kit, we receive only the information you approve:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): your TikTok display name, avatar, and a unique account ID, so you can see which account a video will be posted to.
  • Access tokens: secure keys provided by TikTok that allow the App to upload and publish videos on your behalf (video.upload and video.publish).

We also store the information you enter in the App, such as videos you select, captions, hashtags, privacy settings, and scheduled posting times.

We never ask for or store your TikTok password.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

  • Show you which TikTok account is connected and where each video will be posted.
  • Upload and publish the videos you choose, at the times you schedule.
  • Show you the status of your posts (for example, processing, published, or failed).

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

  • Your videos, captions, schedules, and access tokens are stored locally on your own computer.
  • Videos are sent directly to TikTok only when they are published, using TikTok's official Content Posting API.
  • We do not upload your videos or personal data to our own servers.

4. Sharing With TikTok

When you publish a video, the video and its details (caption, hashtags, privacy setting) are sent to TikTok. TikTok's handling of your content is governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including keeping access tokens on your own device. However, no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as your TikTok account is connected to the App or as needed to publish your scheduled posts. When you disconnect an account, its access tokens and related data are deleted from the App.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Disconnect: You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time from within the App, or by removing the App's access in your TikTok settings (Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions).
  • Data deletion: You can request deletion of any data related to your use of the App by emailing us at Amandaincom@gmail.com. We will complete your request within 30 days.
  • Access: You can contact us to ask what information the App holds about you.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for anyone under 18 years old, or under the minimum age required by TikTok in their country. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will show when changes were made. Continuing to use the App after changes means you accept the updated Privacy Policy.
